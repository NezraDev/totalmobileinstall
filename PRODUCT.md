# Total Mobile Install

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React, Vite, JavaScript, React Router, CSS variables, MapLibre GL JS. Frontend only. The user's planning document selects this stack and the current request authorizes implementation and delegated design decisions.

## Users

New Orleans drivers seeking auto glass, calibration, tint, or detailing services. Visitors need to understand available services and call or request service easily on a phone.

## Product Purpose

Present the family-owned business, show real supplied vehicle work, and collect inquiries. Requests require team confirmation and are never confirmed reservations.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Six services, public routes, local photographs, genuine supplied before-and-after pairs, accessible forms and navigation, and an Our Work map with an anonymized New Orleans overview pin and photo-proof popups. A separate Resend email backend is included for Render; see EMAIL_SETUP.md. No private credentials, CMS, payments, uploads, client addresses, or visitor geolocation are included. Service coverage and unsupported claims must not be invented.

## Brand Commitments

Original icon plus code-rendered TOTAL MOBILE INSTALL wordmark on a white header, as requested in the latest revision. Deep blue #011A82, royal blue #011BCA, red #D30103. The user's latest revision combines the opening into an automatically advancing photographic hero with a translucent blue overlay, replacing the earlier separate gradient and slideshow. The hero fills the first screen, advances every 4.5 seconds without pause/arrow controls, and is followed by scroll-animated source metrics. The user-confirmed shop is at 2642 Tulane Ave, New Orleans, LA 70119, with latitude 29.961365651200683 and longitude -90.09014810481109. Headline: Windshield Repair, At Your Door. Readable modern sans-serif and subtle premium transitions. All images in public.

## Evidence on Hand

Source: ../total-mobile-install-website-planning-prompt.md. Original logo exports and supplied local service and matched before-and-after photography. Six supplied customer testimonials. Statistics are client-supplied and require confirmation before public launch.

## Product Principles

- Make booking and calling easy to find.
- Preserve supplied facts, names, testimonials, and service limitations.
- Show real work and never fabricate proof.
- Keep unavailable integrations honest and useful.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard navigation, persistent form labels, visible focus, reduced motion, readable text, automatically advancing hero, and responsive mobile layout.
